Fitbit Data Access

Privacy policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

Your data remains yours.

With your permission, Fitbit Data Access reads activity, sleep, heart-rate and GPS exercise-route data through Google's API for personal use. It does not modify your original records.

A hosted service processes records and stores account identifiers, authorization tokens and selected summaries in cloud storage. Data may also be cached on your device and visible to other approved accounts. Providers support hosting, authentication and storage. Technical logs may contain account references. Stored records remain until manually deleted.

Data is not sold or used for advertising. Google API Limited Use requirements apply. This site has no analytics; its host may log visits. Revoke access through Google or request deletion at salazar.prime007@gmail.com. Revoking access does not delete existing copies. Updates appear here.
